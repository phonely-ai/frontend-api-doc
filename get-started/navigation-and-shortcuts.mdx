Phonely’s navigation is designed to help you move around the platform faster, switch agents more easily, and pin the tools you use most often. Instead of relying only on the left sidebar, you can use global search, keyboard shortcuts, and a customizable sidebar to get where you need to go with fewer clicks.

This guide covers three key navigation features such as search, keyboard shortcuts, and how you can customize your sidebar.  Together, these features help you:

- Open pages faster
- Switch agents from anywhere
- Discover pages that may not be visible in the default sidebar
- Personalize the sidebar for your workflow
- Use AI when search does not find a page or result

The sidebar stays clean by default, but not every page is pinned there. That is where search and customization become especially useful.

Use these features when you want to:

- Quickly jump to a page without browsing the sidebar
- Search for and switch to a specific agent
- Access hidden pages such as Call Paths, Data Tables, or Call Events
- Pin or unpin features in the sidebar
- Add external pages as custom shortcuts

## **Command \+ K**  Search

Global Search lets you search across pages, tools, settings, and agents from one place. It is the fastest way to move through the platform without manually opening each navigation section.

You can open Search in either of these ways:

- Click the **Search** field in the left sidebar
- Use the keyboard shortcut **Command \+ K** on Mac

<Frame>
  ![Globalsearch](/assets/globalsearch.png)
</Frame>

When search opens, a centered navigation panel appears. At the bottom of the panel, Phonely shows quick reminders for the keyboard controls:

- **↑↓ navigate**
- **↵ open**
- **esc close**

Search helps you quickly find and open pages across the Phonely platform without manually browsing the sidebar. You can use it to open general pages such as Home, optimization pages such as Performance, Call Paths, and Data Tables, and build pages such as Agent Design, Knowledge Base, Workflows, and Agent Settings.

It also lets you navigate to account and support pages, including Account Settings, Profile, Plan & Billing, Notifications, Manage Agents, Manage Numbers, Customize Sidebar, and Help Center.

In addition to pages, Search can also be used to find specific agents. Once you find the agent you want, you can open it directly from the search results.

If your search does not match a page or agent, Phonely shows a fallback state such as nothing found for “...”.  In that state, you will also see: **Ask AI instead.**

## Customizable Sidebar

The default sidebar is intentionally kept focused on the pages most people use often. However, Phonely includes many more pages and tools than what appears there by default.

The **Customize Sidebar** feature allows you to choose which pages appear in the sidebar. This is especially useful for power users who want faster access to more of the platform.

Customize the sidebar when you want to:

- Pin pages you use often
- Expose advanced features that are hidden by default
- Simplify the sidebar for your own workflow
- Reduce navigation friction for repeated tasks

You can reach **Customize Sidebar** in two ways:

#### From Search

## Custom shortcuts

In addition to built-in pages, Phonely also supports **custom shortcuts**.

A custom shortcut lets you add your own navigation item to the sidebar using a URL. This is useful when you want faster access to tools or pages you regularly open outside of Phonely, such as an external dashboard, internal documentation, a project tracker, a CRM, a company homepage, or any other platform your team uses alongside Phonely.

A custom shortcut creates a new sidebar item that links directly to the URL you provide. This helps turn the sidebar into a more complete workspace launcher, especially when your workflow depends on both Phonely and external systems.

Use a custom shortcut when you repeatedly leave Phonely to open the same external tool.
