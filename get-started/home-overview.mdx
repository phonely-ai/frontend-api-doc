The Home page is the first thing you see when you log in to Phonely. It combines a conversational AI interface with a real-time overview of your call activity, so you can ask questions, launch workflows, and monitor performance from a single screen.

![Home Intro Section](/images/home-intro-section.png)

## AI Chat

At the top of the Home page, you'll find a prompt that reads **"What do you want Phonely to do?"** along with a personalized welcome message.

You can type any question or instruction into the chat input — for example:

- "Why are customers dropping off?"
- "Build me an agent"
- "Update my call flow"

Quick-action chips below the input let you jump straight into common tasks without typing.

## Your Overview

Below the chat section, a divider labeled **Your Overview** introduces the analytics portion of the page. Everything below this line reflects your selected filters.

#### Date Range

Click the date-range button to choose a time window. Preset options include:

- **Today**
- **Last 7 days** (default)
- **Last 14 days**
- **Last 30 days**
- **Last 90 days**

You can also pick a custom start and end date from the calendar. Once selected, all metrics and charts refresh to match.

#### Agent Selector

Use the agent selector on the right to view data for one, several, or all of your agents. By default every agent is included.

### Metrics

Four summary cards appear directly below the filters:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Calls Answered** | Total calls handled across selected agents and date range |
| **Minutes** | Aggregate talk time for the period |
| **Cost Saved** | Estimated savings compared to human agents |
| **Resolved** | Count of calls that matched your selected outcome tags |

![Home Overview Section](/images/home-overview-section.png)

Clicking a metric card navigates to the relevant detail page (Call History, Performance Analytics, or Billing).

### Recent Calls

The **Recent Calls** panel lists the most recent conversations with:

- The caller's phone number
- The call outcome (e.g., _Greeting Hangup_, _DO_ZIP_)
- A sentiment icon (positive, neutral, or negative)
- Call duration and time elapsed

Click **View all** to open the full Call History, or click any row to open the call detail modal.

### Call Volume

The **Call Volume** chart plots inbound and outbound call counts over the selected date range. Use it to spot traffic trends, busy periods, or unexpected drops.

Click **View analytics** to navigate to the Performance page for deeper analysis.

## Live Calls

In the top-right corner, a **live calls** indicator shows how many calls are active right now across your selected agents. Click it to see details about each active call.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

At the bottom of the page you'll find quick-reference shortcuts:

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Cmd K` | Search |
| `Cmd J` | AI chat |
| `Cmd S` | Settings |
| `?` | All shortcuts |
