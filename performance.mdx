Performance turns an agent's call history into a board of cards that help you understand traffic, results, and recurring patterns.

<Note>
  Performance reports on the agent's real phone traffic. Web calls are excluded from every card, drilldown, and filter, and **Call type** does not offer that value.
</Note>

Open **Performance** and choose an agent. The page is a single board: every chart, table, and big number on it is a card you can arrange, retype, and drill into.

<Note>
  Data Tables and Proactive Monitoring are no longer separate tabs. Existing data tables are converted to cards the first time you open the page, and a one-time note tells you which view received them. Proactive Monitoring opens in a side panel from the account menu or a report link.
</Note>

## Filter the calls

The date range in the header applies across the active view. Open **Settings** (the gear), then **Call scope** to filter the board by call status, call type, or the Phonely phone number. Use **Date & time** in the same panel to refine the days and hours included; the panel identifies the timezone used for those refinements.

The gear indicates when scope refinements are active. Clear the relevant selections in Settings when you want the full view's scope again. These board-wide controls apply to every card in the view.

For a question about particular outcomes, topics, end reasons, sentiments, analytics fields, or flow blocks, open the card in **Explore** and use **Only count calls where…** to set its own conditions. Save a filter when you want to reuse the same definition across cards. For example, define which calls count as a booked appointment, then use that definition on each relevant card.

A flow-block filter offers three operators: **Reached**, **Never reached**, and **Dropped off**. Use **Dropped off** to count only the calls that ended on a block, which pairs with [Opportunity Analysis](/call-history/overview#understand-why-callers-left-early) when you are investigating where callers leave early.

A **Numbers** filter bounds any numeric field with **is over**, **is at least**, **is under**, or **is at most**, and the bound can take decimals. Use it to count, for example, calls longer than 30 seconds or calls costing over $1.50.

Older board-wide outcome, topic, end-reason, sentiment, caller-number, duration, and flow-block filters are no longer retained. Review older views before comparing their totals with earlier reports, and recreate supported conditions on the relevant cards.

### Compare with another period

Use the clock-arrow control beside the date range, labeled **Compare to another period**, to compare the board with an earlier window.

1. Choose **Previous period** for the window immediately before the current range.
2. To compare with a different period, choose its first day on the calendar and select **Apply**. Phonely keeps the comparison the same length as the current range; for example, compare seven days with an earlier seven days. The comparison must end before the current range begins.
3. Check the period shown on the comparison control before interpreting the results. Open the control and select **Stop comparing** to turn comparison off.

## Read the cards

**Add widget** opens a dialog that splits into chat, templates, and goals views. Before your first guided request, describe your business goal and north star metric—the main measure of success you want to track—then select **Save and continue**. Describe the card you want and review the proposal before adding it. Use **Change business goal and north star metric** in the dialog to revise those answers later.

You can also pick a preset directly. On an empty view, you can ask Phonely to suggest a starting board built from the agent's own flow and outcomes. Preset cards cover common questions:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Calls over time** | Call volume across the selected range |
| **Total calls** | A single number for the range |
| **Average call length** | Mean call duration |
| **Calls by outcome** | Calls grouped by the outcome recorded by the flow |
| **Outcome detail** | Outcomes with supporting metrics in a table |
| **Unique customers** | The number of distinct customers, deduplicated by phone number |

<Note>
  A unique count deduplicates by customer phone number within each time bucket or breakdown group. A customer who appears in more than one group is counted once in each, so grouped unique counts do not add up to an overall customer total. Pie charts and stacking are unavailable for unique counts.
</Note>

Select a segment, point, row, or big-number card to open the matching calls. This lets you move from an aggregate pattern to the conversations behind it.

Each card shows when its data was last updated. Hover a number to see the count or share behind it.

### Change how a card looks

Open a card's **More options** menu to switch between **Time series**, **Bar chart**, **Big number**, **Metric bars**, **Top list**, **Table**, **Call list**, and **Pie**, where compatible. Unavailable types are greyed out with instructions for unlocking them. **Metric bars** requires no breakdown and at least two displayed metrics that share a unit. **Call list** lists the individual calls a query matches; it needs a metric and no breakdown, and is the default for a bare count with no breakdown.

An existing **Sankey** card keeps its own chart type. You cannot convert an ordinary card to Sankey from this menu.

Switching the type changes the presentation only. It never changes the number a card computes.

### Arrange the board

- Drag a card to reorder it.
- Drag a card's edge or corner to resize it.
- Select **Edit in Explore** (the pencil) to change the question behind a card.
- Use **More options** to duplicate a card, copy it to another view, export its data, or remove it from the view.

Layout changes belong to the active view. If a board cannot be saved, the header shows a **Not saved** warning; select it to retry.

You can also add a card from the board's own empty space: a **+** tile in the last row's leftover columns opens the same **Add widget** dialog.

## Build your own card

Select **Add widget**, then **Build a widget manually** to open **Explore**.

Explore replaces the board while you work in it. Build a question by choosing what to count, how to break it down, and which calls to include:

| Part | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Entity and measure** | What each number counts, such as calls or a saved metric |
| **Breakdown by** | What each series, slice, or row represents |
| **Filters** | Which calls contribute to the result |

A metric card reads as a query and then arithmetic: the population row (which calls) sits above the function row. The aggregation menu is split into **By number of calls** and **By value per call**, **Add Metric** offers **Numbers** to add a “calls over …” measure in one pick, the percentile options fold into one **Percentile** row, and a summed metric is captioned **Total …**. The breakdown-by field picker opens on the field it already holds and pins each family heading while its fields scroll.

Measures include a billing family alongside call metrics: **Billed minutes**, **Billed transfer minutes**, and **Cost** (rendered as USD).

Save the result to add it to the board. Use the browser's Back button or the back arrow to leave Explore; if you have unsaved work, Phonely asks before discarding it.

You can save a metric or filter to reuse it across cards. Manage those saved definitions from Explore or from **Settings > Saved metrics & filters** on the board. Deleting a saved definition asks for confirmation and identifies the metric or filter to be removed.

### Use per-call stats

Per-call stats are available as measures, filters, and formula variables, grouped into families such as conversation turns, caller timing, transfers, and silence. A stat's picker row shows a short summary of its note, with the full note on hover. A measure's own picker offers **Numbers** and category stats.

Choose an aggregation that matches the question:

- Sum additive stats, such as counts or cumulative durations, when you want a total across calls.
- Use averages or percentiles to compare per-call values. Per-call averages, maxima, and percentiles cannot be summed; averaging a per-call percentile describes the typical call's percentile, not the percentile across every individual event.

**Transfer hold time**, formerly **Transfer duration**, is the caller's cumulative wait on hold across warm-transfer attempts, including failed attempts. It does not measure time talking to the transfer recipient and has no value for cold transfers.

An unavailable stat value displays as **—**, rather than zero. A date range that starts before a stat's collection began has incomplete coverage. Phonely omits the period comparison when either range has incomplete coverage, rather than showing a misleading change.

## Edit Performance with Ask AI

While **Performance** is open, use [Ask AI](/key-concepts/ask-ai) to add, edit, or remove cards; create, rename, duplicate, reorder, or delete views; or propose changes to saved metrics and filters. State the view and business question you want to change, such as "Add a card showing booked appointments over time."

Review each proposal's preview, affected view, and definition before accepting it. Adding or editing a card offers **Add to board** or **Apply change**; other proposals show the action they will perform. Reject a proposal you do not want. Asking for a change does not apply it automatically.

Use the proposal's **Undo** action when available to reverse an accepted change. You can also open a proposed card in Explore to refine it manually. If you accept an edit while that card is open in Explore, inspect the updated preview and save from Explore when it is ready.

Changing a saved metric or filter can affect cards that use it. Ask AI cannot delete a saved definition while cards still reference it; update those cards first.

## Work with views

A view keeps a board's cards, filters, and settings for an agent. Use the view selector to switch between views and manage them. To change the active view's name or description, open **Settings > View settings**.

Use separate views for questions that need different scopes, such as weekly operations, negative-sentiment calls, or a specific outcome. Give each view a name that describes the question it answers.

## Interpret the results

- Use **outcomes** to measure results recorded by flows or updated after a call.
- Use **topics** to understand what callers discussed.
- Use **end reasons** to understand how calls stopped.
- Open the underlying calls before treating a change on a card as a product or flow issue.

<Tip>
  Keep the date range and filters consistent when comparing results. A change in scope can look like a change in agent performance.
</Tip>
