title: "Usage Notifications "
description: "Learn how to set up usage notifications in Phonely to receive email alerts when your workspace reaches key usage thresholds and avoid unexpected overages."
---

Usage notifications help you stay in control of your Phonely spending by alerting you when your workspace usage reaches specific thresholds.

A usage notification sends an email alert when your Phonely workspace reaches a defined percentage of its monthly usage limit. Notifications are informational only, they do not stop calls or restrict agents, but they give you early visibility so you can adjust workflows, pause campaigns, or upgrade plans if needed.

You can create multiple notifications at different thresholds (for example, 50% and 75%) to progressively track consumption throughout the billing cycle.

## Create a Usage Notification

To access usage notifications:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings in your Phonely workspace.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="From the click Notifications.">
    <Frame>
      ![Notificationssettings](/assets/notificationssettings.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="You’ll see the Notifications page, where all usage alerts are created and managed.">
  </Step>
</Steps>

If no notifications are configured yet, Phonely will prompt you to add one.

Click **Add Notification** to begin setting up a new alert.

In the first step, you’ll choose the alert type. Select usage alert, which is designed specifically for monitoring monthly usage limits.

Next, choose one or more usage threshold percentages. These represent the point at which the alert will trigger. Choices include 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100%, depending on how early you want to be notified.

<img
  src="/assets/adding-usage-notifications.png"
  alt="Adding Usage Notifications"

  style={{ width:"74%" }}
/>

Once you’ve selected a threshold, continue to the next step.

### Add Notification Recipients

In the final step, you’ll specify who should receive the alert.

Enter one or more email addresses for the people responsible for monitoring usage, this might include administrators, billing contacts, or operations managers. Each email added will receive a notification when the selected threshold is reached.

When finished, click **Create Notification** to activate the alert.

Your notification becomes effective immediately and applies to the current and future billing cycles.

## Manage Usage Notifications

Once a usage notification is created, you can manage it at any time from the notifications page. This includes updating thresholds or recipients, removing notifications you no longer need, and reviewing when alerts were triggered.

### View Existing Notifications

All configured usage notifications appear as cards on the Notifications page. Each card shows:

<Expandable title="Details">
  - The usage threshold (for example, 75% usage)
  - The notification type (Usage)
  - The list of recipients who will receive the alert
</Expandable>

### Edit a Notification

You can update an existing notification if your usage strategy or recipients change. To edit a notification, click the edit (pencil) icon on the notification card.

### Delete a Notification

If a notification is no longer needed, you can remove it entirely. Click the delete (trash) icon on the notification card and confirm the action.

### View Notification History

Phonely keeps a record of when usage notifications are triggered, making it easy to audit alerts or confirm delivery.

To view this history, click the clock icon on a notification card. A Notification History panel opens, showing all alerts that have been sent for that notification.
