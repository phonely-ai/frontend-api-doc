The plan & billing page allows you to view your current subscription, track your usage, manage plan upgrades or downgrades, and review billing history. This page ensures you have full visibility into your organization's call time, overage costs, and invoices.

<Note>
  Your personal account settings—including name, avatar, phone number, password, and API key—are also managed from the **Plan & Billing** tab under Account Settings.
</Note>

### Managing Your Plans

At the top of the page, you’ll find details about your **Current Plan**, including:

- **Plan Type:** Indicates your current subscription tier (e.g., Free, Starter, Professional, Business, or Enterprise).
- **Status:** Shows whether your plan is _active_, _incomplete_, or _expired_.
- **Usage Summary Bar:** Displays the total minutes used out of your included monthly allowance.
- **Renewal Date:** The date when your plan will automatically renew.
- **Manage Plan:** Click this button to view or modify your subscription.

### Total Usage Summary

This section provides a breakdown of how your organization is using its minutes and estimated call time savings.

It includes:

- **Total Usage:** Total number of minutes used during the current billing period.
- **Total Overage:** The number of minutes used beyond your plan’s included quota.
- **Estimated Monthly Usage:** A projection of your monthly call activity based on current patterns.
- **Cost Savings Insight:** Displays how much agent time (and equivalent cost) your AI agents have saved.

![Total Usage Summary](/assets/total-usage-summary.png)

<Tip>
  Monitoring this section helps you predict when you may need to upgrade your plan to avoid overage charges.
</Tip>

### **Billing History**

The billing history section shows a record of your invoices and payments.

You’ll see:

- **Invoice Date:** The date the invoice was issued.
- **Status:** Indicates whether an invoice is paid or pending.
- **Amount:** The total billed amount for that period.
- **Invoice:** Opens a detailed invoice document in a new window.
- **Usage Report:** Provides a breakdown of how minutes were consumed across agents and call types.

## Billing Error Notifications

When there's an issue processing payment for your subscription, a red banner appears at the top of your workspace. This banner alerts you that your billing information needs to be updated to prevent service interruption.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look for the red banner with the message 'Unable to process payment: Please update your billing information to prevent interruption of service.'">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the 'Update Payment Info' button in the banner.">
    {/* TODO: Add screenshot showing the billing error banner with the Update Payment Info button */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="You'll be redirected to the Plan & Billing settings where you can update your payment method.">
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you don't update your billing information, your service may be interrupted. The banner will remain visible on all pages (except the Workflows tab) until the payment issue is resolved.
</Warning>
