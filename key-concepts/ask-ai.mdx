Instead of switching between docs, videos, and trial-and-error, Ask AI lets you ask questions exactly where you're working and get answers tailored to your current context.

Ask AI is available across the entire Phonely platform — including the workflow canvas, call history, performance dashboards, knowledge base, guidelines, and navigation. Look for the floating Ask AI button in the bottom corner of any screen.

## Ask AI Modes: Assistant and Test

Ask AI has two dedicated modes at the top of the panel.

### Assistant

The Assistant tab is your all-in-one AI partner for building agents and understanding how they work. You can ask it to design entire agents, modify workflows, analyze call data, manage knowledge bases, update guidelines, or answer questions about both the platform and your specific agent — all in the same conversation.

**Building agents**: Ask the Assistant to create or modify agents and their workflows:

- "Build me a customer support agent that handles returns and exchanges."
- "Add an escalation path if the order lookup fails."
- "Set up a post-call email summary for every completed appointment."

**Getting help**: Ask the Assistant about the platform or your agent's current behavior:

- "Why is the agent moving on without asking for the user's phone number?"
- "What languages does the agent understand right now?"
- "What's the difference between live-call and post-call actions?"
- "How do call outcomes differ from call topics?"

**Analyzing calls**: Ask the Assistant to review call data directly:

- "Why are callers hanging up during the greeting?"
- "Summarize the last 50 calls and flag any issues."
- "Which calls this week resulted in a booked appointment?"

Ask AI can analyze up to 100 calls at a time. When a selection exceeds what fits in the analysis window, the assistant tells you exactly how many calls were included so you always know the scope of the answer.

### Confirmation Before Changes

When you ask the Assistant to make a change — like modifying a workflow block, updating a guideline, or editing knowledge base content — it restates what it's about to do and asks you to confirm before proceeding. This ensures you stay in control and nothing changes without your approval.

### Test Mode

The Test tab simulates your agent like a real call. You'll see the agent's greeting (for example, _"Thank you for calling Phonely! How can I help you today?"_ and can interact as if you're a caller.

You can:

- Type responses to simulate caller input OR switch to talk to speak to it
- Restart tests instantly
- Observe how prompts, branches, and logic behave in real time

This makes Ask AI a powerful companion to test your agent, helping you debug conversational issues before publishing.
