---
title: "Reduce Spam Labeling Risk"
description: "Operational checklist for reducing outbound spam-labeling risk when using Phonely campaigns."
---

## Checklist

Use this checklist before launching an outbound campaign:

1. Attach the correct outbound phone number.
2. Verify consent and contact-source quality.
3. Set sane retry and cadence rules.
4. Test the first-call experience before full launch.
5. Review call outcomes after launch and adjust quickly if answer rates or completion rates degrade.

## Rules

- ALWAYS respect consent and compliance requirements for the target campaign.
- NEVER launch a large campaign before validating the flow with a smaller sample.
