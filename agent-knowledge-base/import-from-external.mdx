title: "External Sources"
description: "Connect to external knowledge platforms like LivePro to seamlessly import existing documents and make them available for your AI agent."
---

You can connect Phonely to external platforms to automatically import your existing documentation and make it available for your AI agent. This is especially useful if your company already maintains a centralized knowledge base elsewhere, such as LivePro.

![Import From Other External Gi](/assets/import-from-other-sources.gif)

<Steps>
  <Step title="From the left sidebar, go to Agent Design > Knowledge Base, then switch to the Knowledge Base tab. ">
    Here, you’ll see all your data sources, including uploaded and created documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="At the top of the page, click Import from External.">
    A modal will appear displaying available integration options - for now, Phonely supports importing from LivePro.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select LivePro and click Connect.   ">
    You’ll be prompted to enter the following details:

    - Domain: Your LivePro workspace domain (e.g., `https://your-domain.com`).
    - API Key: Your LivePro API key to authorize the integration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Once filled, click Continue to connect your account.">
    <img
      src="/assets/connecting-to-live-pro-new.png"
      alt="Connecting To Live Pro New"
      style={{ width:"84%" }}
    />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Import Your Documents

After connecting successfully, Phonely will retrieve your LivePro documents.\
You can then select which files to import into your data sources list.

Your imported documents will appear alongside your existing ones, ready for your AI agent to use in answering questions.
