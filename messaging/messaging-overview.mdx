Messaging lets your agents continue conversations outside a live phone call. Use the **Messaging** settings tab to prepare SMS, review upcoming chat and email channels, and control how long SMS conversations stay active.

## Available Channels

| Channel | Status | What you can configure |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **SMS** | Available for supported regions | A2P 10DLC registration, attached phone numbers, and SMS session duration. |
| **Chat** | Coming soon | A web chat channel for agent conversations. |
| **Email** | Coming soon | A threaded email channel for agent conversations. |

## SMS Session Duration

Session duration controls how long an SMS thread stays part of the same active conversation before Phonely starts a new one. Choose the length that matches how quickly your customers usually reply.

Available durations:

| Duration | Use it when |
| :-- | :-- |
| **30 minutes** | SMS replies are expected immediately, such as active support conversations. |
| **1 hour** | Customers may step away briefly before responding. |
| **4 hours** | Conversations often pause during the same business day. |
| **12 hours** | Customers may reply later in the day. |
| **24 hours** | Most follow-ups happen within a day. |
| **3 days** | Longer back-and-forth is common. |
| **7 days** | Customers often respond several days later. |

Changes to session duration save automatically.

## Messaging in Call History

Call rows can show an **AI SMS** badge when the conversation includes an AI messaging session. Conversations may also use mixed call types when a customer journey includes both voice and messaging activity.
