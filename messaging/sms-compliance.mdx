---
title: "SMS Compliance"
description: "Understand SMS compliance status for Phonely and imported phone numbers."
---

SMS compliance shows whether a phone number is ready to send SMS through your registered messaging campaign. Use it to confirm which numbers can support AI SMS before enabling SMS follow-up or messaging workflows.

## Where to Check SMS Compliance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your agent.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Agent Design > Settings.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Phone Numbers.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the SMS Compliance column in the phone numbers table.">
  </Step>
</Steps>

The table shows **Yes** when the number is SMS-compliant and **No** when it is not ready for SMS.

## Number Owner Badges

Each phone number shows whether it is a **Phonely Number** or an **External Number**.

| Owner | How SMS compliance works |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Phonely Number** | Complete A2P 10DLC registration in **Settings > Messaging**. Approved Phonely numbers are managed through the Phonely registration flow. |
| **External Number** | Complete verification with the external provider, such as Twilio, then refresh the compliance status in Phonely. |

## Refresh External Number Compliance

Imported numbers can be refreshed after you complete verification with the external provider.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the actions menu for the external phone number.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Refresh SMS Compliance.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the table to update the SMS Compliance value.">
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Refresh SMS Compliance** is only available for external numbers. Phonely numbers use the A2P 10DLC registration status from **Settings > Messaging**.
</Note>
