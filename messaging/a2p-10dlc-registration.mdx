A2P 10DLC registration helps carriers verify who is sending SMS messages and why. Complete this setup before using Phonely numbers for agent SMS in supported regions.

</Note>

## Before You Start

You need at least one phone number assigned to an agent before you can register for SMS. If the status card asks you to assign a number first, add or assign a phone number from your agent's **Phone Numbers** settings.

Prepare these details before opening the registration form:

| Detail | Examples |
| :-- | :-- |
| Business identity | Legal business name, registration number, business type, industry, website, and business address. |
| Contact person | Name, email, phone number, title, and job position for the person responsible for SMS registration. |
| Campaign use case | Customer Care, Account Notifications, Marketing, Two-Factor Auth, Public Service Announcement, or Mixed. |
| Message details | Campaign description, sample messages, opt-in flow, opt-in confirmation, and HELP response. |
| Compliance links | Privacy policy URL and terms and conditions URL. |
| Phone numbers | The numbers that should be attached to the approved SMS campaign. |

After you submit, the status card changes to **Pending review**. Carrier review usually takes 1 to 7 business days. Phonely emails you when the registration is approved or rejected.

## Registration Statuses

| Status | What it means | What you can do |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Not registered** | No registration has been submitted yet. | Click **Configure** and submit your business and campaign details. |
| **Pending review** | Phonely received your registration and queued it for carrier review. | Wait for the review result. Registration details are locked while review is active. |
| **In review** | Carriers are reviewing your brand and campaign. | Wait for a final decision. |
| **Approved** | Your SMS campaign is approved. | View the configuration. New Phonely numbers are attached to the campaign as they are added. |
| **Rejected** | The registration needs changes before it can be approved. | Review the rejection reason, then click **Edit and resubmit**. |

## Admin Review

Admins can open **Admin review** from the SMS registration area when a submitted registration is available. From the review modal, admins can inspect the brand and campaign details, mark the registration **In review**, **Approve** it, or **Reject** it with a rejection reason.

Use rejection reasons to tell the submitter exactly what needs to change, such as missing opt-in language, mismatched business information, or incomplete compliance links.
