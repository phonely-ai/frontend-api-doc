The voice & personality tab allows you to customize how your AI agent sounds and behaves during a conversation. These settings help create a more natural, brand-aligned voice experience, whether you want your agent to sound professional, friendly, or casual.

You can control:

- The background environment (office ambiance).
- The agent’s tone and communication style.
- Whether to include human-like speech fillers.
- How your agent pronounces words through a Phonetic Dictionary.

## Add Office Noise

This feature adds light background sound to mimic a real-world environment, making your agent’s voice sound more authentic and less “studio-perfect.” It’s useful when you want your conversations to feel natural and comfortable for the caller.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Voice & Personality tab.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Under add office noise, click the dropdown menu.">
  </Step>
</Steps>

- **None (default):** No background noise; your agent’s voice is clean and clear.\
  Best for formal or quiet interactions such as customer support or technical assistance.
- **Office Space:** Adds a realistic office background sound (e.g., subtle typing or chatter).\
  Ideal for professional or corporate support environments.
- **Soft Office Space:** Adds a gentler ambient noise for a natural but calm tone. Best for relaxed or friendly interactions like onboarding or sales calls.

### Available Personalities

You can choose from several tone presets:

| **Personality** | **Description** | **Best For** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Casual** | Friendly, relaxed, and conversational. | Informal support, onboarding, or friendly brand tone. |
| **Humorous** | Adds light humor and playfulness to responses. | Marketing, entertainment, or brand awareness calls. |
| **Direct** | Straightforward and efficient, focuses on clarity. | Technical support, B2B or productivity-driven calls. |
| **Formal** | Polite, structured, and business-like tone. | Corporate clients, enterprise-level or compliance contexts. |
| **Persuasive** | Encouraging and goal-oriented communication. | Sales calls, conversions, or lead follow-ups. |
| **Friendly** | Warm and empathetic, suitable for relationship-focused brands. | Customer care, service recovery, or hospitality brands. |

<Tip>
  You can experiment with different tones for different call workflows (e.g., “Formal” for support, “Friendly” for sales follow-ups).
</Tip>

## Phonetic Dictionary

The Phonetic Dictionary lets you control exactly how your agent pronounces specific words. This is useful for:

- Brand names (e.g., “Phonely”)
- Acronyms (e.g., “HIPAA”, “API”)
- Foreign names or domain-specific terms

Phonely uses **IPA (International Phonetic Alphabet)** notation. Under the hood, pronunciations are passed to the voice engine as IPA phonemes formatted with pipe-separated symbols in double angle brackets (e.g., `<<ˈ|dʒ|ə|m|ˈ|b|ə|ˈ|l|aɪ|ˈ|ə>>` for jambalaya).

### How to Add an Entry

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your agent and go to Agent Settings > Voice & Personality.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to the Phonetic Dictionary section.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="In the Word field, type the word you want to adjust.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="In the Phonemes field, enter the IPA pronunciation.">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the + button to save the entry.">
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Writing IPA Phonemes

IPA phonemes represent individual sounds. For example, the word “Phonely” can be represented as `<<ˈ|f|oʊ|n|l|i>>`.

<Tip>
  Use this feature to maintain consistent pronunciation for your brand and technical terms.
</Tip>

### Three-Field Entry: Word, Pronunciation, Phonemes

Each dictionary entry now has three inputs instead of two, giving you a friendlier way to capture pronunciation alongside the raw IPA:

| Field | What it's for | Example |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Word** | The word in your script that should be re-pronounced. Matched in agent output. | `Phonely` |
| **Pronunciation** | Your own phonetic respelling — a memo for yourself or your team in plain letters. | `foh-nuh-lee` |
| **Phonemes** | The IPA phonemes the voice engine actually uses. Auto-suggested from the pronunciation. | `<<ˈ|f|oʊ|n|l|i>>` |

Only the **Phonemes** field changes how your agent speaks at runtime — the **Pronunciation** field is saved alongside it as a human-readable reference and is not used by the voice engine.

### Auto-Suggesting Phonemes

You don't have to write IPA by hand anymore. As you type into the **Pronunciation** field, Phonely runs a phoneme generator in your browser and fills in the **Phonemes** field with a suggested IPA respelling — already formatted with the `<<...>>` and pipe separators the voice engine expects.

If the first suggestion isn't quite right, click the **regenerate** icon next to the Phonemes field to cycle through alternative variants:

- **American English** pronunciation
- **British English** pronunciation
- **Scottish English** pronunciation

Duplicate suggestions are skipped automatically, so you only see distinct options. You can also edit the Phonemes field directly at any time — manual edits take precedence over the suggestions.

<Tip>
  The phoneme generator covers most English words. For names, made-up brand terms, or anything the generator struggles with, type the closest English-sounding spelling into **Pronunciation** (for example, `kar-tee-zhuh` for *Cartesia*) and let the suggestion fall into place.
</Tip>

### Preview a Pronunciation in Place

Next to the **Phonemes** field, an inline **Play** button previews just the current word with your in-progress IPA — so you can hear how it sounds before saving the entry. The preview runs at normal speed with background sound off, so what you hear is the pronunciation in isolation.

<Tip>
  Use the inline Play button to iterate on tricky words. Tweak the IPA, hit Play, repeat — no need to save the entry and run a full test call.
</Tip>
