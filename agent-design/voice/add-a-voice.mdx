---
title: "Add a Voice "
description: "Learn how you can import voices into phonely"
---

The **Add Voice** feature lets you import or connect external voices (for example, from 11 Labs) into your Phonely Voice Library. You can then use these imported voices in your agents and workflows.

<Note>
  Clicking **\+ Add Voice** in the voice library opens an **Add or Clone a Voice** chooser first. Pick **Add by Provider ID** to import a voice by ID (covered on this page), or pick **Clone from Audio** to record or upload a sample for cloning (see [Clone a Voice](/agent-design/voice/clone-voice)). The chooser slides smoothly into the selected sub-flow, and a back arrow returns you to the chooser.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Voice Library" titleSize="h3">
    Go to the **Voices** section of your Phonely dashboard.

    You’ll see:

    - **Featured Voices** - curated, ready-to-use AI voices.
    - **Explore All Voices** - all available voices, including imported and cloned ones.

    Click the **\+ Add Voice** button on the upper right or beneath the “Explore All Voices” section.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/assets/explore-all-voices.png"
        alt="Explore All Voices"
        title="Explore All Voices"
        style={{ width:"88%" }}
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Provider" titleSize="h3">
    In the **Add a Voice** window, open the **Provider** dropdown.

    Select your preferred provider, such as **11 Labs**.

    - Phonely currently supports third-party providers like _11 Labs_ for realistic and multilingual voice options.

    <img
      src="/assets/how-to-add-a-new-voice.png"
      alt="How To Add A New Voice"
      title="How To Add A New Voice"
      style={{ width:"59%" }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a model" titleSize="h3">
    After choosing a provider, open the **Model** dropdown and select the voice model you want to use.

    For example:

    Under **11 Labs**, available models may include:

    - **eleven\_multilingual\_v2**
    - **eleven\_flash\_v2\_5**
    - **eleven\_turbo\_v2\_5 (default)**

    Under **Cartesia**, available models may include:

    - **sonic-2**
    - **sonic-3 (default)**

    Choose the model that best fits your use case. Different models may vary in speed, quality, responsiveness, or multilingual support.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/assets/select-model.png"
        alt="Select Model"
        title="Select Model"
        style={{ width:"53%" }}
      />
    </Frame>

    Use this setting to control how the provider generates speech for that voice. Different models may vary in speed, responsiveness, or multilingual support.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Voice ID" titleSize="h3">
    After selecting the provider, you’ll see an **Add ID** field.

    Paste your **voice ID** from the provider’s platform (e.g., from your 11 Labs account).

    - This ID uniquely identifies the voice you want to import.

    Double-check that the ID is correct to ensure the voice loads successfully.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Language" titleSize="h3">
    Choose the **Language** in which the voice will operate.

    Available options may include: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Hindi, Dutch, Swedish, and Danish.

    Select the language that best matches your voice model or intended audience.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/assets/select-voice-language-1.png"
        alt="Select Voice Language 1"
        title="Select Voice Language 1"
        style={{ width:"58%" }}
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and Add the Voice" titleSize="h3">
    Review your selections (Provider, ID, Language).

    Click **\+ Add Voice** to finalize the process.

    The new voice will now appear in your Voice Library under _Explore All Voices_.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage and Use the New Voice" titleSize="h3">
    Once added, you can:

    - **Preview** the voice by clicking the play button.
    - **Set it as default** by clicking **Select**.
    - **Add to Favorites** by tapping the heart icon ❤️.
    - **Edit settings** (e.g., tone, speed, or pitch) using the gear icon ⚙️ if available.
  </Step>
</Steps>
