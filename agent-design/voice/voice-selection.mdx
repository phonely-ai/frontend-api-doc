Phonely lets you select, test, and personalize the voice your AI agent uses during calls. You can choose from a library of professionally designed voices, filter them by language, gender, or accent, and even fine-tune their delivery for your use case.

Phonely allows you to clone a custom voice so your AI agent can sound more personalized or closely match your brand’s tone. More details on [how you can clone your voice here](/agent-design/voice/clone-voice).

From the agent design page, locate the choose a voice section. Click the voice button to open the Voice Library.

This brings up two main sections:

- **Featured Voices:** Curated, popular voices that showcase different styles and accents.
- **Explore All Voices:** A searchable, filterable list of every available voice.

Each voice card includes:

- A Play button to preview how it sounds.
- Tags showing its accent, gender, and supported languages.
- A heart icon to mark it as a favorite for quick access later.

Click **Select** to assign the voice to your AI agent. The selected voice will show a “Selected” badge under actions.

Searching and Filtering Voices To find the perfect match for your AI agent:

**Search any voice:** Use the search bar at the top to quickly locate a specific voice by name.

**Filters:** Click Filters to narrow results.

<Expandable title="Filters">
  - Language: e.g., English, Spanish, French, Hungarian.
  - Gender: Male or Female.
  - Accent: e.g., American, Australian, British.
  - Provider Tags: Such as 11Labs, OpenAI Realtime, or \_Multi-Lingual. \_
</Expandable>

You can combine filters to fine-tune your search (for example, _Female_ \+ _English_ \+ _11Labs_).

## Customize a Voice

Once a voice is selected, click the **Settings** cogwheel next to it to open voice customization.

Here, you can fine-tune how the AI sounds during calls:

- **Speed:** Adjust the speaking pace using a slider.
- **Tags Overview:** Review key traits such as language, provider, or model type.
- **Multi-Lingual Support:** Toggle this settings on to enable the AI to speak and understand multiple languages seamlessy.

When done, click **Save Settings** to apply your customizations.

## Manage Voices

You can:

- [**Add New Voices**](/agent-design/voice/add-a-voice)**:** Use the **\+ Add Voice** button to upload or import new voices from providers such as 11 Labs.
- **Mark Favorites:** Click the heart icon to save preferred voices.
- **View Usage Metrics:** Review voice usage metrics to understand call volume, duration, and overall AI voice consumption.

## Filter to Your Voices Only

The **Mine** filter pill on the voice library toolbar narrows the all-voices table to voices you own — anything you added via **Add by Provider** or cloned from audio. Each matching row also shows a **Mine** badge so you can spot them at a glance when the filter is off.

A voice counts as "yours" if you created it directly, or if it belongs to your organization. Public, featured, and platform-provided voices are excluded from this view.

<Tip>
  Use the **Mine** filter to quickly find a voice you just added without scrolling through the full library.
</Tip>

## Try a Voice Before You Commit

The voice picker includes a **Test Agent** panel that lets you preview the currently focused voice with your agent's own configuration — without leaving the picker or saving any changes.

{/* TODO: Add a screenshot of the Test Agent panel with the waveform visualization and editable sample text. */}

The panel uses:

- **Editable sample text** — defaults to your agent's greeting, but you can type anything up to 500 characters to hear how the voice handles your specific phrasing.
- **The agent's phonetic dictionary** — any pronunciations you've configured are applied during preview, so brand names and technical terms sound the way you want them to.
- **Office noise mix** — when you've set a background ambient sound, the preview plays with that mix so the voice sounds the way it will in production.
- **Your selected speed** — the speed slider value is applied to the preview, letting you audition pacing changes in real time.

Click **Play** to hear the voice; a 3-second scrolling waveform shows the audio in motion. Edit the sample text and play again to compare results without leaving the screen.

## Speed: Per-Provider Ranges

The speed slider's effective range adapts to the voice you have focused, because each TTS provider supports a different speech-rate window:

| Provider | Speed Range | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| ElevenLabs | 0.7 – 1.2 | Narrow window; best for natural pacing. |
| Cartesia | 0.6 – 1.5 | Wider range; supports both slow and brisk delivery. |
| Rime | 0.5 – 1.5 | Widest range; can go very slow or very fast. |

`1.0` is normal speed on every provider. When you switch voices between providers, your saved speed is remapped so the *relative* position around `1.0` is preserved — a speed near the top of ElevenLabs's range will land near the top of Cartesia's or Rime's range after switching, rather than getting clipped or reset.

<Tip>
  If you don't see your previously saved speed exactly mirrored after switching to a new provider, that's expected — the slider position is remapped proportionally so the perceived pacing stays consistent.
</Tip>

After customizing, your chosen voice appears at the top of the **Agent Design** screen as the active voice. This voice will be used for all calls made by your agent.

<Tip>
  For best results, test multiple voices to find one that fits your brand personality and communication tone.
</Tip>
