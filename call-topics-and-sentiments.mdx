Call Topics and Sentiment allow Phonely to automatically classify and analyze conversations. These classifications help you understand why customers are calling, how they feel, and how effectively your agent is resolving requests.

Call Topics identify _what the caller wanted_, while Sentiment identifies _how the caller felt_. Together, they provide powerful insights that improve analytics, reporting, and agent optimization.

Call Topics and Sentiment serve three primary purposes:

- Automatically classify calls based on intent and outcome
- Allow manual review and adjustment in Call History
- Enable advanced analytics and comparison in the Performance page

Once configured, Phonely automatically detects topics and sentiment during conversations and makes them available across analytics, filtering, and reporting tools.

## How to add a Call Topic

Call Topics define the types of conversations your agent handles. These help Phonely identify caller intent and organize analytics around meaningful categories.

1. Open your agent
2. Click **Settings**
3. Select the **Call Tagging** tab
4. Locate the **Call Topics** section
5. Click **Add Topic**
6. Enter the topic name and the topic description. The description is where you define what happens for the call to be 
7. Click **Add**

![Call Tagging Settings](/assets/call-tagging-settings.png)

## How Call Topics Are Detected Automatically

During each conversation, Phonely analyzes the caller’s intent and applies relevant topics automatically.

Once the call ends, these topics become part of the call record and appear in:

- Call History
- Performance analytics

## Viewing Call Topics in Call History

The call history and Analytics dashboard provides a centralized view of all calls.

Each call entry includes the duration, call outcome, sentiment, call topic, and timestamp. 

To review or edit detected topics:

1. Go to **Analytics**
2. Click **Call History**
3. Click on any call

This opens the **Highlights page**.

![Call Tags Call Stats](/assets/call-tags-call-stats.png)

In the Highlights page, under the **call stats section**.

You can:

- View automatically detected topics
- Add additional topics
- Remove incorrect topics
- Search and select topics

Example topic selector includes:

- callback requested
- caller transferred
- appointment booked
- order status inquiry

Changes update analytics instantly.
